The challenge

Stitch Fix is an online styling service that advertises on a lot of social platforms. For Meta, the ads had to bring in new clients, speak to women and men differently, and keep up with a steady calendar of promotions. That meant statics, video and dynamic product ads (DPA).

The approach
The offer stays the same.

In the campaign shown here, every static has the same lime-green “$20 off your first Fix” badge. Whatever the outfit or headline, people can see the offer right away.

Different hooks, same offer.

Each ad led with something different: the season (“Spring style made easy”), a joke (“Side effects may include compliments”), news (“Big + Tall is here”) or reassurance (“All style, no risk”).

Women’s and men’s each got their own look.

Women’s ads used styled outfit collages. Men’s included a flat lay with the Stitch Fix box. Statics were square, and the videos were 9:16 vertical or 4:5.

Keeping it on schedule.

I also handled production timelines and asset tracking, and kept review rounds moving so the ads went out on time.

What I learned

With a steady calendar of promotions, the schedule matters as much as the idea. Clear timelines and review rounds are how good ideas actually go out on time.

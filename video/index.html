How I make video

I do every step myself: the idea, the script, the filming and the edit. Brands get a finished video they can post, and nothing gets lost between steps.

The idea.

For wool&, they asked for a Short and the idea was mine: one dress, styled three ways. For my own channel, I laid a long video out in chapters: what a digital garden is, where it comes from, how it helps, how creatives can use one and how to start.

The script.

I write every script. For the Short, the structure was the idea itself, the same dress in three looks. For the long video, the script follows the chapters, and the timestamps in the description let viewers jump to the part they want.

The filming.

I shoot the footage myself, and I’m on camera for wool& and for my channel. For Under Luna, I filmed the product in use.

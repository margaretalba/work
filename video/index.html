Video and creator content

I script, film and edit my own videos, for brands like wool& and Under Luna and for my YouTube channel. My wool& Short reached 459K views.

How I make video

I do every step myself, from the idea to the final edit, so brands get a video that’s ready to post.

The idea.

I start by thinking about the customer and the company’s goal, then look for an idea that serves both.

The script.

Writing for video is an exciting challenge for me. I want it to feel conversational, not like an obvious ad.

The filming.

I plan my shots ahead of time, and make sure they feel native to whatever brand I’m working with.

Editing

I edit in Premiere, and use After Effects and CapCut when I need them.

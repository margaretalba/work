LTK × Madewell In-App Sale

The challenge

In October 2023, LTK and Madewell ran a 20% off sale that was only in the LTK app. Creators needed to be ready to post, shoppers needed to hear about it, and it all had to feel like both brands at once.

The approach
Two brands, one event.

Shoppers needed to trust it was really Madewell, and creators needed to see it as an LTK moment. I helped set the creative direction so every piece felt like both.

Instantly recognizable.

A sale only works if people notice it. One mark on every asset meant each email, post and story built on the one before it.

A countdown.

All assets were designed by me, including all the posts leading up to the sale, so interest kept building instead of peaking on day one.

Making it easy for creators.

Creators drive the sales on LTK, so the more prepared they were, the better the sale would do. I gave them checklists, content ideas and a reason to care: creators who share sale content earn 115% more on average.

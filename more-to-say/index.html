The challenge

In 2024, LTK’s founder and CEO, Amber Venz Box, started a podcast and needed branding for it. It had to fit LTK, stand on its own and work across platforms.

The approach
Part of LTK, but its own show.

For a podcast to grow, people need to follow the show, not just the company. Keeping LTK as a small sign-off let the show’s name and Amber lead.

A logo with personality.

A podcast cover competes with thousands of others. The wordmark needed enough personality to stand out, without fighting the host’s portrait.

The same look everywhere.

People find a podcast in a lot of places. One look across the cover, YouTube and social, matched to Amber’s existing presence, meant listeners would recognize it anywhere.

Teaser, then reveal.

A quiet “Coming soon…” story built curiosity before launch, so the reveal had an audience waiting for it.

Built for small sizes.

Most people see a podcast as a tiny square in an app, so I made sure the cover still works at that size.

Results

One look across YouTube, social and digital.

The show has a 4.9 rating from 35 ratings across Apple Podcasts and Spotify, per Podnews (October 2026).

What I learned

I learned how to build on a brand that already exists, and how to make a design flexible enough to work everywhere the show shows up.

LTK × adidas In-App Sale

The challenge

In April 2023, LTK and adidas ran their first-ever sale together, only in the LTK app. It had to reach creators, who needed to get ready to post, and shoppers, who had four days to buy.

The approach
Easy to spot.

In a busy feed, a sale gets about a second to register. A repeating #LTKxadidas ribbon in adidas black and white made every asset read as part of the same event.

Two audiences, two messages.

Creators and shoppers needed different things at different times. Creators had to be ready before the sale, and shoppers needed a reason to show up on day one, so each got their own assets and timing.

Only in the app.

The 30% off code lived only in the LTK app, and the sale had a firm end date. That gave shoppers a reason to open the app, and a reason not to wait.

Creators up front.

I built every asset around creator photos and handles, so the sale felt like a recommendation from someone you follow, not another brand ad.

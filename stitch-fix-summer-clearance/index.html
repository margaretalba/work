Stitch Fix Summer Clearance

I created the email, site, app and social for Stitch Fix’s Summer Clearance, across the women’s, men’s and kids’ lines. The team made its email layout the template for future clearance promos.

The challenge

Most of my promotions run everywhere at once. Summer Clearance had to reach women, men and parents across email, site, app and social, with one offer that landed the same way everywhere.

The approach
Let the offer do the work.

A clearance shopper is deciding one thing: is the discount worth a look? So the offer is the biggest message in every piece, and the email is short enough that nothing competes with it.

Each channel has its own job.

Email and social have to stop someone mid-scroll, so they lead loud with the discount. The site and app reach people who are already shopping, so there the offer fits into the experience instead of interrupting it.

One offer, every audience.

The women’s, men’s and kids’ versions share the offer and the look, so the sale reads as one event wherever someone sees it.

Results

It was a top-performing email for both the women’s and men’s lines, and the team made its layout the template for future clearance promos.

What I learned

A promo that runs everywhere needs one clear idea. Once the offer was the hero, every channel only had to show it in its own way, which is what made the layout easy to reuse.

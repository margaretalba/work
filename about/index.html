How I got here

When I was younger, all I wanted was to be an artist. My parents had never met an artist who made a living at it, and didn’t believe one existed. So we compromised, and I went to school for marketing. Then I fell in love with it. Marketing gave design a reason, and made it feel more thoughtful and meaningful to me.

After school, I built a career in graphic design, working in tech and retail, both in-house and at an agency. On the side, I did freelance marketing for local B2B companies.

I think my design background makes me a better marketer, especially now, when standing out matters more than ever.

Right now

I’m at Stitch Fix, where I design promotions and growth assets and make sure they go out on time. See the Meta work.

Before that

I was at LTK until May 2024, working on in-app sales with brands like Madewell and adidas. I made the emails, social posts, banners and decks for those sales, and I designed the look of LTK’s podcast, More to Say. I also got to work on campaigns for Abercrombie & Fitch and ASOS.

Before that, from 2017 to 2019, I was the Marketing & Design Coordinator at Project Object and Upper Metal Class in Portland. I did a bit of everything there: the store identity, events, emails and the Shopify site. See Project Object.

Video

I also script, film and edit videos, for brands like wool& and Under Luna and for my own YouTube channel. See the videos.

Outside work

Away from marketing, I’m a metalsmith and I teach wax carving. You can see that side of my work at margaretalba.com.
